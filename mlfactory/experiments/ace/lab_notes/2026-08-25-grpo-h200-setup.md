## Diagnosis of slow smoke (probe measurements, GPU1, non-intrusive)

User flagged the smoke looked slow (~25 min, first group still
running). Diagnosis — **not hung; the regime is genuinely slow under
HF SDPA** (probe script `/workspace/probe_throughput.py`, run on the
idle GPU1 while the smoke kept running on GPU0):

- `attn_implementation: sdpa`; **flash-attn-2 not available** in the
  image; hybrid linear-attention blocks use transformers' torch
  fallbacks (the revisit trigger named in `ENVIRONMENT.md` has now
  been hit: throughput).
- Measured throughput (batch 4, thinking on, bf16, 9B, H200):
  - short context (≤1k): **~151 tok/s aggregate** (~38 tok/s/seq)
  - warmup into ~6k context: **~82 tok/s aggregate**
  - i.e. per-token cost grows with context (8 full-attention blocks
    are O(T) per token); deep-trace extrapolation puts an all-26k
    group at ~30–45 min. First smoke group elapsed time is consistent
    with traces near the cap.
- GPU0 during generation: 33–35% util, ~185 W, 1980 MHz — busy,
  memory-bandwidth/architecture-bound, not compiling or wedged.
- Smoke design flaw (corrected for next script): logging per-group
  gives zero visibility during long groups; production scripts need
  streaming progress.

Forecast implication: generation dominates the GRPO wall-clock even
more than first estimated; infrastructure levers (flash-attn / FLA
kernels, TP=2) or a reduced rollout cap are the dials.
